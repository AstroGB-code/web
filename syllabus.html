UPSC Prelims

The Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination is the first stage. It consists of two objective-type papers.

Paper I: General Studies (200 Marks)

  • Current events of National and International importance.
  • History of India and Indian National Movement.
  • Indian and World Geography - Physical, Social, Economic Geography of India and the World.
  • Indian Polity and Governance - Constitution, Political System, Panchayati Raj, Public Policy, Rights Issues, etc.
  • Economic and Social Development - Sustainable Development, Poverty, Inclusion, Demographics, Social Sector Initiatives, etc.
  • General issues on Environmental ecology, Bio-diversity and Climate Change - that do not require subject specialization.
  • General Science.

Paper II: CSAT (200 Marks) - Qualifying

Qualifying Paper with minimum marks fixed at 33%.

  • Comprehension.
  • Interpersonal skills including communication skills.
  • Logical reasoning and analytical ability.
  • Decision making and problem-solving.
  • General mental ability.
  • Basic numeracy & Data interpretation (Class X level).

UGC NET (Paper 1)

Detailed syllabus for the General Paper on Teaching & Research Aptitude (Paper-I).

Unit-I: Teaching Aptitude

Teaching: Concept, Objectives, Levels of teaching (Memory, Understanding and Reflective). Characteristics and basic requirements. Learner’s characteristics: Adolescent and adult learners. Factors affecting teaching. Methods of teaching: Swayam, Swayamprabha, MOOCs. Evaluation Systems.

Unit-II: Research Aptitude

Research: Meaning, Types, and Characteristics. Positivism and Post-positivistic approach. Methods: Experimental, Descriptive, Historical, Qualitative and Quantitative. Thesis and Article writing. ICT in research. Research ethics.

Unit-III: Comprehension

A passage of text is given. Questions are asked from the passage to be answered.

Unit-IV: Communication

Communication: Meaning, types and characteristics. Effective communication: Verbal and Non-verbal, Inter-Cultural and group communications, Classroom communication. Barriers to effective communication. Mass-Media and Society.

Unit-V: Mathematical Reasoning and Aptitude

Types of reasoning. Number series, Letter series, Codes and Relationships. Mathematical Aptitude: Fraction, Time & Distance, Ratio, Proportion and Percentage, Profit and Loss, Interest and Discounting, Averages etc.

Unit-VI: Logical Reasoning

Arguments, Mood and Figure, Formal and Informal fallacies, Uses of language, Venn diagram. Indian Logic: Means of knowledge. Pramanas: Pratyaksha, Anumana, Upamana, Shabda, Arthapatti and Anupalabddhi.

Unit-VII: Data Interpretation

Sources, acquisition and classification of Data. Quantitative and Qualitative Data. Graphical representation (Bar-chart, Histograms, Pie-chart, Table-chart and Line-chart) and mapping of Data. Interpretation of Data.

Unit-VIII: ICT

ICT: General abbreviations and terminology. Basics of Internet, Intranet, E-mail, Audio and Video-conferencing. Digital initiatives in higher education. ICT and Governance.

Unit-IX: People, Development and Environment

Sustainable development goals. Human and environment interaction. Environmental issues: Local, Regional and Global. Air, Water, Soil, Noise and Waste pollution. Climate change. Natural and energy resources. National Action Plan on Climate Change.

Unit-X: Higher Education System

Institutions of higher learning in ancient India. Evolution of higher learning in Post Independence India. Oriental, Conventional and Non-conventional learning programmes. Professional, Technical and Skill-Based education. Policies, Governance, and Administration.

CSIR NET

Part A: General Aptitude

This part shall contain questions pertaining to General Aptitude with emphasis on logical reasoning, graphical analysis, analytical and numerical ability, quantitative comparison, series formation, puzzles etc.

Part B & C: Subject Specialization

  • Chemical Sciences
  • Earth, Atmospheric, Ocean and Planetary Sciences
  • Life Sciences
  • Mathematical Sciences
  • Physical Sciences

BPSC (Bihar)

Preliminary Examination (150 Marks)

  • General Science.
  • History of India and History of Bihar.
  • Geography of the World and Geography of Bihar (Rivers etc).
  • Indian Economy and Bihar Economy.
  • Indian Polity and Post-independence Bihar.
  • Indian National Movement and Bihar's role in it.
  • General Mental Ability.

MPPSC (Madhya Pradesh)

The MPPSC State Service Exam is conducted in three stages: Prelims, Mains, and Interview.

Preliminary Exam

  • Paper I: General Studies (History, Geography, Polity & Economy of India & MP).
  • Paper II: General Aptitude Test.

NDA / NA

Mathematics (Class 12th Level)

Algebra, Trigonometry, Analytical Geometry, Calculus, Vector Algebra, Statistics and Probability.

General Ability Test

  • Part A: English Vocabulary, Grammar, and Usage.
  • Part B: General Knowledge (Physics, Chemistry, Bio, History, Geography, Current Events).
