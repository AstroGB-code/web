UPSC Prelims Pattern & Syllabus

The UPSC Prelims comprises two objective type papers (General Studies I and General Studies II or CSAT) for a total of 200 marks. Both papers are usually held on the same day in two sessions via offline mode (pen-paper).

  • Negative marking of -0.66 marks for every incorrect answer (GS Paper-1).
  • Negative marking of -0.83 marks for every incorrect answer (CSAT).
  • The cut off for Prelims 2020 was 92.51 for General Category.

Paper-1 Syllabus:

  • Current events of national and international importance.
  • History of India and Indian National Movement.
  • Indian and World Geography-Physical, Social, Economic Geography.
  • Indian Polity and Governance.
  • Economic and Social Development.
  • General issues on Environmental Ecology, Biodiversity.
  • General Science.

UPSC Mains Examination

The UPSC Mains exam is the second stage of the Civil Services Examination. Candidates who score above the cutoff in GS Paper I and more than 33% marks in GS Paper-II (CSAT) are eligible to appear.

The Main test follows a pattern of 9 theory papers (essay descriptive type). Only 7 are counted for merit ranking. The maximum marks for the UPSC Mains exam are 1750.

  • Marks are given for "relevant, meaningful, and succinct answers."
  • Tests basic understanding, analytical ability and view on conflicting goals.
  • Total candidates allowed is 12-13 times the number of vacancies.
